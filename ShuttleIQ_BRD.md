Business Requirements Document (BRD) - ShuttleIQ Version: 1.0 Purpose:
Define business requirements for ShuttleIQ, an AI-powered badminton
training and tournament management platform for India. Executive Summary
ShuttleIQ is a web, mobile, and desktop platform focused on tournament
organizers while serving players, coaches, academies, and parents. The
system supports BWF-compliant tournaments and AI-driven player
development. Business Objectives • Simplify badminton tournament
operations. • Provide player training and performance insights. • Enable
BWF-compliant fixture generation. • Offer nutrition, warm-up, recovery,
and injury prevention guidance. • Build a nationwide badminton
ecosystem. Stakeholders Primary: Tournament Organizers. Secondary:
Players, Coaches, Academies, Parents. Tertiary: Umpires, Referees,
Sponsors, Administrators. Scope In Scope: - Tournament Management -
Fixture Engine - Player Profiles - Rankings - Match Statistics - AI
Style Analyzer - Training Planner

-   Diet & Recovery
-   Notifications Out of Scope (Phase 1):
-   Wearable integrations
-   Marketplace
-   International federations Functional Requirements FR1: Create and
    manage tournaments. FR2: Support Singles, Doubles, Mixed Doubles
    (Men/Women). FR3: Generate fixtures using Knockout, Round Robin,
    Swiss and Hybrid formats. FR4: Maintain player rankings. FR5: Record
    match scores and statistics. FR6: Provide AI-based training
    recommendations. FR7: Generate player performance reports. FR8: Send
    notifications and reminders. Tournament Requirements • BWF rules
    compliance. • Seeding support. • Court allocation. • Referee
    assignment. • Live score updates. • Certificates and reports
    generation. Player Module Player profile management. Match history.
    Statistics dashboard. Training recommendations. Nutrition guidance.
    Warm-up and recovery routines. Coach Module

Monitor player progress. Assign training plans. Review videos and
performance. Provide feedback. AI Features • Playing Style Analysis. •
Personalized Training Plans. • Match Outcome Prediction. • Video
Analysis. • Opponent Analysis. • Injury Risk Prediction. • AI Coach
Assistant. Non-Functional Requirements Availability: 99.5% Security:
Role-based access control. Performance: Fixture generation under 30
seconds. Scalability: Support 100,000+ users. Auditability: Log all
critical actions. Technology Stack Frontend: React, Flutter, Electron.
Backend: .NET 9 APIs. Database: PostgreSQL. Caching: Redis.
Infrastructure: Docker, Kubernetes, Azure. Success Metrics 50,000
players. 500 organizers. 2,000 tournaments.

100,000 matches managed. 25,000 monthly active users. Release Roadmap
Release 1: Tournament Engine, Rankings, Notifications. Release 2: AI
Training, Diet, Coach Dashboard. Release 3: Video Analytics and Injury
Prediction.
